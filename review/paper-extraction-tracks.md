# Paper-sized extraction tracks

These are dissemination cuts of the knowledge base, not alternate scientific
claims. Each track reuses the same claims ledger, generated witnesses, figures,
and unresolved boundaries. The HTML book remains the authoritative context.

## Track A — Certified pruning of multiconductor parallel limits

**Lead result.** Fixed linear multiconductor parallel members can retain one
member's current-limit family while proving another member's limits redundant
through a normalized quadratic or complex-polydisc containment test. This is
constraint pruning, not asset aggregation.

**Core sources.**

- `TR-PAR-005`, `TR-PAR-006`, `TR-PAR-007`;
- [Multiconductor parallel AC decision case](../docs/src/cases/multiconductor-parallel-ac-decision.md);
- [Non-proportional four-wire case](../docs/src/cases/four-wire-parallel-ac-decision.md);
- [Four-wire nominal-pi case](../docs/src/cases/pi-four-wire-parallel-ac-decision.md);
- generated certificates and independent reproduction artifacts.

**Figures.** Complex-plane feasible-set geometry, case-escalation grid, and the
parallel decision witnesses.

**Boundary.** Singular maps, state/control-dependent members, joint implication
across several retained limits, and global nonlinear optimality remain open.

## Track B — A representation contract for power-network graphs

**Lead result.** Simple graphs, directed/oriented multigraphs, port--factor
models, and asset/dependency relations are query-relative views connected by
typed maps and preservation contracts rather than one universal abstraction
ladder.

**Core sources.**

- `THESIS-001`, `ARCH-PORT-001`, `GRAPH-CYCLE-001`, `TR-GRAPH-001`,
  `TR-GRAPH-002`;
- [Formal representation frameworks](../docs/src/foundations/formal-representation-frameworks.md);
- [Maps between representation frameworks](../docs/src/foundations/representation-maps.md);
- [Cycles, parallelism, and radial structure](../docs/src/foundations/cycles-parallelism-radiality.md).

**Figures.** Map of maps, bus-meaning overlays, cycles/parallelism/radial tail,
active-state radiality, and provenance lineage.

**Boundary.** Conductor-terminal lifts, checked open-system composition, and
state-conditioned multi-terminal compilation remain research extensions.

## Track C — Guarded reductions from Kron/Ward to executable certificates

**Lead result.** Boundary elimination, calibrated Ward equivalents, and
optimized structural reductions are distinct transformations. A reduction is
decision-usable only when its observation family, recovery map, error chain, and
refusal conditions are explicit.

**Core sources.**

- `TR-KRON-001`, `TR-KRON-002`, `TR-KRON-003`;
- [Kron, Ward, and optimized network equivalents](../docs/src/transformations/kron-ward-opti-kron.md);
- [Certificate schema and composition](../docs/src/transformations/certificate-schema-and-composition.md);
- [Guarded normalization rules](../docs/src/transformations/guarded-normalization.md).

**Figures.** Kron fill-in, certificate composition, guarded-rule gate, and the
argument spine.

**Boundary.** Extended Ward support injections, singular maps, nonlinear
solver-exported KKT diagnostics, and a general optimizer-independent theorem
remain open.

## Shared extraction protocol

Each paper cut should retain the claim IDs in its manuscript source, cite the
generated witness filenames, reproduce the relevant tests from a clean checkout,
and state the same exact/inner/outer/scenario classification used by the book.
No track should introduce a stronger theorem merely by omitting the book's
boundary paragraphs.
