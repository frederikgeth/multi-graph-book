# Archived review working documents

These files preserve the internal response and preparation notes from the
August 2026 expert-review pass. They are provenance for the development
history, not formal publication requirements and not part of the reader-facing
HTML or PDF route.

The active research records remain one level up: the raw reviews, search
protocol, evidence matrix, bibliography audit, and dated search runs. The
roadmap records which external-validation items remain optional future work.

## October 2026 review series

witness scripts, and their evidence JSON files were removed from the working
tree after the final review. They remain in git history at commit `5edc71c`
(`git show 5edc71c:review/<file>`). The final review and its evidence stay in
